# Dinety 

Dinety is a free rythme game.

---

## Introduction 

Dinety is separated into **Plot Mode** and **Single Mode**. 
You can solve puzzles using the clues provided to advance the game’s storyline and unlock the corresponding songs in the **Plot Mode**. It's around Dinety's plot, about dimensions and the universe. 
We will be including a wide variety of songs, as well as tracks taken from other games, in the **Single Mode**, and we will do our utmost to make the note charts as engaging as possible.
